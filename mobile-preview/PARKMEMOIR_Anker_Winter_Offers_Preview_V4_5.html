Show sizing assumptions

Required rated capacity ≈ daily energy × days between reliable full recharges × 1.25 reserve ÷ 0.85 delivery factor. The 0.85 factor is a planning assumption, not a lab efficiency measurement for either product.

If you have a guaranteed full recharge each day, the calculation uses 1 day even if your trip is longer. Uncertain winter solar input is never counted as a guaranteed recharge.

A source's maximum charging/discharging temperature is a device operating limit, not a guarantee of runtime at that temperature. Check continuous AC output and appliance starting surges separately.

03 · WINTER SOLAR REALITY

Will a 200W panel help?

Yes, when conditions permit. But winter daylight, shade and battery charging temperature matter more than the panel's nameplate rating.

Don't assume 200W all day

The panel's 200W figure is a rated power value under specified conditions, not a daily energy budget.

  • Check solar input compatibility, cable connections and panel positioning.
  • Tree shade, short winter days, clouds and low sun angles reduce output.
  • When the battery is below its allowed charging temperature, do not rely on solar recharge.
  • We do not subtract hypothetical solar input from the battery capacity you need.

    05 · QUICK ANSWERS

    Before you camp

    Why might a 2kWh S2000 not suit my 12V fridge?

    The S2000 manufacturer specifications do not list direct 12V DC output. The C2000 Gen 2 has a 12V output; check plug, current limits and your actual fridge model before deciding. An inverter-and-adapter workaround has additional losses and needs separate safety review.

    Can the C1000 Gen 2 use a battery expansion?

    No. The C2000 Gen 2 with its compatible expansion battery is a different, 4,096Wh bundle in the models compared here. An external alternator charger adds charging access, not battery capacity.

    Can these Anker stations charge below freezing?

    Manufacturer documentation lists a 32°F (0°C) minimum battery charging temperature for the models discussed here. Check the exact model manual, pack temperature and compatible charger. Neither a sunny panel nor vehicle charging overrides the battery temperature limit.

    Does Anker last longer than Jackery in winter?

    Not established by the specifications alone. Anker publishes a lower minimum discharge operating temperature, but a controlled, same-load runtime test would be needed to compare usable battery energy in cold weather.

    Do I need a 1,024Wh station just for phones?

    Usually not when wall charging or a modest power bank meets your needs. For multi-day off-grid use, compare your actual total Wh requirement; the C1000 Gen 2 may be an optional upgrade rather than a necessary purchase.

    Can I use the 200W solar panel as daily recharge?

    Use real measured input, daylight and safe charging temperatures. This tool deliberately treats winter solar as uncertain and does not subtract estimated panel output from your baseline battery requirement.
